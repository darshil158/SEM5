Term Work & Viva Submission Instructions (AY 2026)

1. Strict Timings & Reporting

All students must report in the designated examination laboratory or classroom by 9:00 AM sharp on the scheduled examination date. Late entry will not be permitted under any circumstances.

2. Mandatory College Uniform & ID Card

Students must appear in proper college uniform and possess a valid student identity card displayed around the neck. Students failing to meet dress code requirements will not be entertained.

3. Practical File Index & Certification

Laboratory journal manuals must contain an entirely verified index sheet, bearing the faculty signatures for each completed experiment. The certificate page must be signed by the subject teacher and Head of Department.

4. Evaluation Scheme (MCQ & Oral)

The internal evaluation consists of an objective MCQ examination followed by a one-on-one oral viva examination. Viva marks directly contribute to continuous progressive assessment (PA) and term-work credit.

5. Assignment File Verification

All subject assignments (Assignment 1, Assignment 2, and Assignment 3) must be compiled in the term work file. Viva questions will directly probe concepts covered in assignments.

6. Societal Internship Report

Internship report (BE05000011) must be submitted in the prescribed departmental hardcopy spiral-bound format accompanied by NGO/Organization certificate, logbook of 60 hours, and presentation slides.

🎓 GTU Evaluation & Passing Norms

End Semester Exam (ESE)

70 Marks Theory

Minimum 40% (28 out of 70 marks) required in the university written examination to clear the theoretical component of the course.

Progressive Assessment (PA)

30 Marks Mid-Term

Derived from the departmental Mid-Semester Examination (typically covering Units 1, 2, 3), assignments, and classroom quizzes.

Term Work / Practical (V / I)

50 Marks Lab & Viva

Evaluated during laboratory sessions and external viva. Minimum 40% (20 out of 50 marks) required to pass practical coursework.
